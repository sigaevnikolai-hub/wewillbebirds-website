HER Console

Material Lacquered MDF

The Story

I don't really have project favorites. But some leave a dent in my heart. This one is that.

From the first drawing to the final install, I was excited to make it real. I believe results like this only happen when client and builder truly understand each other — when there's trust, freedom, and real communication on both sides.

Smooth and sleek on the outside. Fun and rich on the inside — like a well-tailored Paul Smith suit.

And a huge mirror with a precise cutout to fit the console. No buttons. Light appears with a glide of your hand along the curve. All sensors hidden, tracking motion in silence.

Really proud of this one. Will miss her.

The exterior

Fluted lacquered fronts. Glass top. Every surface flush — nothing interrupts the line.

The mirror

Custom cutout to the millimeter. The console and mirror read as one object.

The interior

Hand-painted drawer boxes. Brass inlay trim. A private world inside a composed exterior.
