Sleep & Energy

Sleep Optimization (Most Important)
To wake at 7 AM refreshed:
• In bed by 10 PM = 9 hours (adjust based on need)
• No screens 30 min before bed
• Room dark and cool
• Consistent bedtime - even weekends (mostly)

Track it:
Use Garmin sleep tracking, see patterns, adjust bedtime based on data

Most tiredness = bad sleep, not supplements
Supplements (Week 4 - After Routine is Solid)
Don't start yet - build the routine first, then add if needed.

Potential options to explore:
• Vitamin D - If low (get blood test first). Energy, mood, immune.
• B12 - Energy, especially if not eating much meat.
• Magnesium - Better sleep quality → better energy. Take at night.
• Creatine - Consistent energy, no crash. 5g daily. Also helps gym.

Week 4 plan:
1. Get blood work (Vitamin D, B12 levels)
2. Start with magnesium at night (helps sleep)
3. Add others based on blood work
4. Track energy levels for 2 weeks

Not magic - Fix sleep, morning routine, gym first. Supplements are 10% of the equation.
Why You're Tired (Current Diagnosis)
Likely reasons:
1. Waking up too late - Sleeping past natural rhythm
2. Poor sleep quality - Not enough deep sleep
3. Morning jerking off - Drains energy early
4. No structure - Decision fatigue all day
5. Irregular gym - Inconsistent exercise = worse energy

The fix (in order):
Week 1-2: Morning routine, gym 3x/week, better sleep
Week 3-4: Routine is solid, energy improving
Week 4+: Add supplements if still needed

Most people don't need supplements. They need sleep, structure, and movement.

Gym Routine

Days: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday 5-6 PM
Location: Crunch FiDi
Goal: Build consistency, then strength
5 minutes
Warm-up
Light cardio (treadmill walk, bike)
Dynamic stretches
Main Workout (35 min)
Compound Movements (Every Session):
1. Squat or Leg Press - 3 sets × 8-10 reps
2. Bench Press or Push-ups - 3 sets × 8-10 reps
3. Rows or Lat Pulldown - 3 sets × 8-10 reps
4. Overhead Press - 2 sets × 10 reps

Accessory Work (Pick 2):
• Bicep Curls - 2 sets × 12 reps
• Tricep Extensions - 2 sets × 12 reps
• Plank - 2 sets × 30-45 sec
• Calf Raises - 2 sets × 15 reps
5-10 minutes
Cool Down
Light cardio
Stretch
Foam roll if available

Rules

1. Same Days, Same Time
Tue/Thu/Sat 5-6 PM - non-negotiable unless actually sick or injured

2. The 10-Minute Rule
Can't make full hour? Do 10 minutes. Showing up > perfect workout.

3. No All-or-Nothing
Missed Tuesday? Still hit Thursday. One skip ≠ failed week.

4. Pre-Pack Gym Bag
Night before (Mon/Wed/Fri): clothes, shoes, water, towel, headphones

Progression:
Every 2 weeks: add 5 lbs to lifts OR add 1 rep per set

Instagram Pipeline

Monday Routine (1 hour)

• Review all photos/videos from past week
• Check phone, DSLR, DJI Osmo, Insta360
