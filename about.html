Wewillbebirds is a custom millwork studio based in Brooklyn, New York. We work with homeowners, architects, and designers on residential and commercial projects across the city.

Every piece is built to spec, in-house, with the hardware and finishes we believe in. We use Blum hardware because it performs. We finish with Rubio Monocoat and oil-based polyurethane because anything less isn't something we're willing to deliver.

We don't compete on price. We compete on the quality of the result — and the confidence that the design is right.

If you know what you want and you want it done properly, this is where you should be.
