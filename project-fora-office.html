Fora Office

Location Tribeca, New York
Client Fora Travel
Story

The pop-up closed, and we moved the bookcases to their new spot — Fora's permanent office in Tribeca. Feels good to see them keep going.

Same modular shelving from the SoHo pop-up, now integrated into the office space. Exposed brick, LED underlighting, fabric backing. The units work as room dividers and display — books, travel guides, objects that tell the Fora story.

When you build something temporary and it gets a second life, that says something.
