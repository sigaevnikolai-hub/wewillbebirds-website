One day we will be birds

We are full of fear and full of longing. Carrying love, doubt, memory, and all the weight of being human. Wanting to break free. To escape whatever keeps us small.

And still, we are afraid. Afraid of loss. Afraid of change. Afraid that freedom might cost us the only thing worth keeping - Love.

Love moves through us like birds through sky.

It is not something you hold — it is something you are. You do not lose it by growing, by leaving, by becoming.

It lives in you the way grain lives in wood — invisible until you cut deep enough to see it.

We give love to the world through what we create and how we hold each other. Every time you love fully, every time you make something with everything you have, every time you hold someone like it matters — you are flying.

Every time you're standing in the noise and the light and the music with the people you love most in this world — you cross over. You disappear. You are not a body with a name and a fear.

You are infinite.

Life is brief. A spark in the dark. And between those two — everything.

So let people feel your love.

Make something beautiful.

Leave the world lighter than you found it.

Because one day we all will be birds.
