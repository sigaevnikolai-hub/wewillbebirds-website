We Will Be Birds

Right now, we are heavy.

Bound by work, by time, by all the weight of being human.

Afraid. Stuck. Carrying obligations we didn't choose

and grief we can't set down.

We rage against it.

We dream of escape.

We think: what if I could be lighter?

But then the fear arrives—

What if freedom means losing love?

What if being light means being empty?

What if I have to let go of everything real?

You don't.

Love that is real survives transformation.

If it lives in you, it will carry with you—

not as weight, but as wings.

A bird is light.

Untethered. Above the noise. Above the fear.

But it carries love.

Be free, but do not lose love.

Be light, but stay open.

Leave fear—not feeling.

You are not guilty for wanting to be free.

You are not trapped forever.

We are meant for more than this.

Something in us is moving toward lightness.

Toward transcendence.

Toward the moment when death no longer

terrifies us the same way.

Not yet.

But one day.

Let people feel your love.

Give it to the world.

Make this place a little more beautiful

than you found it.

Your life is just a sparkle.
Soon, you will be free.
