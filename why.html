We Will Be Birds

Right now, we are heavy

Weight of work. Weight of time. Weight of responsibility. Weight of being human.

Trapped in obligations, in grief, in what-ifs, in fear.

But something in us is moving

Toward lightness. Toward freedom. Toward release.

Not yet. But one day.

This is transcendence. When death no longer terrifies you the same way. When you realize we are becoming freer than we are now.

The fear

What if freedom means losing love?

What if being light means being empty?

What if I have to let go of what's real?

The answer

You don't.

Real love survives transformation. It stays with you. If it's your love, it will always be within you.

The bird is light, untethered, above the noise, above the weight — but it carries love.

Be free, but do not lose love.
Be light, but stay open.
Leave fear, not feeling.

Let people feel your love. Give love to the world. Make it a better place.

We are not trapped forever

We are meant for more than fear.

We are dreaming. One day, one day.

You're not guilty for wanting to be free.

Your life is just a sparkle.
Soon you will be free.
