We Will Be Birds

We are full of fear and full of longing.

We carry love, doubt, memory, and all the weight of being human.

We want to break free.

We want to rise.

We want to escape whatever keeps us small.

And still, we are afraid.

Afraid of loss.

Afraid of change.

Afraid that freedom might cost us the only thing worth keeping — love.

It won't.

Love does not disappear.

It is not something you hold. It is something you are.

You do not lose it by growing, by leaving, by becoming.

It lives in you the way grain lives in wood — invisible until you cut deep enough to see it.

We give it to the world through what we make, how we live, and how we hold each other.

Life is brief.

A spark in the dark.

And between those two — everything.

Every moment you chose courage over comfort.

Every time you made something beautiful for no reason other than it deserved to exist.

Every hand you held when the easy thing was to let go.

Let people feel your love.

Make something beautiful.

Leave the world lighter than you found it.

One day, fear will loosen its grip.

One day, we will rise.

One day, we will be birds.
